## 1. WHAT IS HAIP

### The One-Sentence Answer (memorise this)

> **HAIP is the AI intelligence and communication layer for Indian hospitality — it unifies every guest touchpoint into one cockpit, answers guests in seconds with AI, and gives owners and managers the data they need to grow revenue and coach their teams.**

### The Longer Version (for internal understanding)

Indian hospitality — homestays, villas, service apartments, boutique hotels, resorts, hotel chains, travel operators — runs on disconnected tools. Guests ping on WhatsApp. High-intent calls come to mobile numbers. Instagram DMs go unanswered. OTA booking enquiries live in Booking.com's extranet. Repeat guests get treated like strangers because no one built a shared memory.

Meanwhile, revenue is leaking invisibly. Missed calls mean missed bookings. Slow replies mean guests book somewhere else. No one knows which agents close best, which channels convert most, or whether the AI Concierge could handle 40% of messages autonomously.

HAIP fixes all of this. It is:

- **A unified inbox** — every message from every channel in one place
- **An AI call intelligence system** — every call auto-transcribed, summarised, and scored
- **An AI Concierge** — drafts WhatsApp replies in your brand's voice, your agents approve with one tap
- **A sales pipeline (Leads360)** — every inquiry tracked from first contact to booking
- **A WhatsApp marketing platform** — broadcast campaigns, automation rules, subscriber management
- **An analytics layer** — conversion rates, agent QA scores, revenue attribution, channel ROI

HAIP sits beside your existing PMS (Hostaway, eZee, Cloudbeds). It does not replace your booking engine, channel manager, or telephony provider. It makes all of them smarter.

### What HAIP Is NOT (prevent mis-selling)

| Not This | Why |
|---|---|
| A PMS or booking engine | Hostaway / eZee remain the system of record for reservations |
| A softphone or calling app | Agents take calls on their personal mobiles via IVR; HAIP captures the recording |
| A channel manager | We sync with Hostaway; we don't push rates to OTAs |
| A CRM that replaces your spreadsheets from scratch | HAIP enriches guest data from real interactions — you don't manually enter data |
| A generic helpdesk SaaS (Zendesk, Freshdesk) | Built exclusively for hospitality workflows and Indian regulatory compliance |

## 2. THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

### Why This Problem Exists

Hospitality in India is a high-touch business at every segment. A 6-villa property in Coorg, a homestay in Kodaikanal, a 40-room business hotel in Pune, a service apartment chain in Hyderabad, or a resort in Wayanad — all share the same core problem: 2–10 staff handling 50–300 guest interactions per day across:

- WhatsApp Business (personal or shared)
- Instagram DMs
- Direct phone calls (personal mobile or IVR)
- OTA inboxes (Booking.com extranet, Airbnb messages)
- Email
- Walk-ins and referrals

No single tool manages all of these. Staff context-switch constantly. Guest history lives in someone's personal WhatsApp. Managers have no visibility into whether enquiries are being followed up. Owners can't see which channel drives actual bookings — only which one drives the most noise.

### The 8 Core Pain Points (use these to open conversations)

**Pain Point 1: The Multi-Tab Circus**
> "Our reservations team has 6 tabs open at once — WhatsApp Web, Booking.com extranet, Airbnb messages, Instagram, email, and our PMS. They lose context every time they switch."

This is the most universal pain. Every property with more than one channel suffers it. Every minute lost to tab-switching is a minute a high-intent guest is waiting.

**Pain Point 2: Missed and Mis-handled Calls**
> "We miss calls after hours. Or the call connects but nobody ever reviews what was discussed — whether the guest was quoted the right rate, whether an upsell was attempted."

Guests call when they're serious — whether it's a ₹8,000 homestay or a ₹3L villa weekend. A missed call is a missed booking at any price point. Properties using personal mobile numbers for reservations have zero visibility into whether those calls are being handled well.

**Pain Point 3: No Guest Memory**
> "Our best guests get treated like strangers the second a different staff member picks up. All the context is in one person's WhatsApp."

Repeat guests expect recognition. When a property can't remember that this guest is celebrating their 10th anniversary, doesn't eat meat, and prefers a ground-floor villa — trust erodes. HAIP builds a persistent guest profile from every interaction.

**Pain Point 4: Invisible Revenue Leakage**
> "We suspect our AI Concierge could handle 40% of enquiries automatically but we have no way to prove it. We also have no idea which OTA enquiries convert vs. direct."

Properties waste money on OTA commissions (typically 15–25%) while having no data on direct booking conversion. They can't make a business case for investing in direct channels without this attribution.

**Pain Point 5: 0% Call QA Coverage**
> "We sample maybe 5 calls a week for quality review. That means 95% of our calls are never reviewed for script compliance, rate quoting accuracy, or missed upsells."

Manual call sampling is impractical at any scale. Properties have no idea if their reservations team is quoting correctly, handling objections well, or missing upsell opportunities on every call.

**Pain Point 6: WhatsApp at Scale Is Broken**
> "We want to send check-in reminders or seasonal offers to our guest list on WhatsApp, but doing it from a personal number means getting flagged as spam. We don't know how to do it officially."

Meta's official WhatsApp Business API is the only legal way to broadcast at scale. Getting set up requires navigating Meta's approval process, choosing a BSP, and understanding template requirements — most hospitality operators have never done this.

**Pain Point 7: DPDP Compliance Is Coming**
> "We record calls and store guest data. We have no idea if we're compliant with India's new Data Protection law."

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) requires consent capture for recordings, data subject rights workflows, and documented retention policies. Most hospitality properties — at any segment — have zero infrastructure for this.

**Pain Point 8: No Coaching Data for Reservations Staff**
> "Our top reservations manager is brilliant. But we don't know what she does differently. We can't train the junior staff to match her results."

Without call transcripts, QA scores, and conversion data per agent, coaching is subjective. HAIP gives every manager a weekly AI digest of who's performing and why.

## 4. THE HAIP PLATFORM

### Module 1: Unified Inbox

**What it does:**
Every guest message from every channel arrives in one place. WhatsApp messages, Instagram DMs, post-call AI wrap-ups, web chat enquiries — all unified into a single chronological timeline per guest, regardless of channel.

**Key capabilities:**
- Auto-routing by language, shift schedule, or channel type
- Assignment to specific agents or teams
- Internal notes per conversation (not visible to guest)
- Read receipts and typing indicators for WhatsApp
- Message templates for quick replies
- Full conversation history even if guest switches from WhatsApp to Instagram DM

**Why it matters to the client:**
> "Your team never says 'I didn't know she'd already asked that on Instagram' again. Every agent sees every interaction."

**What to show in a demo:**
Open a guest conversation showing a WhatsApp thread, an Instagram DM, and a call wrap-up card — all on the same guest timeline.

---

### Module 2: AI Call Intelligence

**What it does:**
Every inbound and outbound call through the client's IVR (CallerDesk or Acefone) is automatically pulled, transcribed, analysed by AI, and stored as a call record in HAIP. No manual work required.

**The call flow (important for explaining):**
1. Guest calls the property's IVR number
2. IVR plays a brand greeting and captures consent for recording
3. Call forwards to the reservations agent's personal mobile (no app, no headset)
4. Normal phone conversation happens
5. After the call, IVR webhook fires to HAIP
6. HAIP pulls the recording, sends to Sarvam AI for transcription (Indian accent optimised)
7. Claude AI generates a wrap-up: summary, sentiment, disposition, next action, missed revenue flags
8. Wrap-up card appears in the HAIP cockpit within ~90 seconds of call end

**Key capabilities:**
- Auto-transcription (primary: Sarvam AI for Indian accents; fallback: Deepgram → Whisper)
- AI wrap-up with suggested disposition (Hot / Quoted / Booked / Lost / Callback)
- QA scoring against the property's rubric (rate quoting accuracy, upsell attempts, tone)
- Missed revenue flags (when AI detects an upsell opportunity was skipped)
- 100% call coverage — no sampling, no manual listening
- Missed call detection and follow-up triggers

**Why it matters:**
> "You currently review 5% of calls. We review 100%. You find out your senior agent is quoting the wrong rate to 3 out of 10 callers. That's ₹4L/month in lost revenue — and you had no idea."

**What to show in a demo:**
Play a sample call wrap-up card. Show the AI summary, the QA score breakdown (rate accuracy, upsell attempt, closing technique), and the missed revenue flag.

### Module 3: AI Concierge

**What it does:**
HAIP drafts contextual WhatsApp replies in the property's brand voice, grounded in the property's own knowledge base (room types, rate cards, package rules, house rules, F&B menu, activities). Agents review and send with one tap — or it runs autonomously on high-confidence replies.

**How it works:**
- Property uploads knowledge base (room descriptions, rate cards, house rules, FAQs)
- AI is trained on this knowledge base and the property's tone
- When a guest message arrives, AI drafts a reply
- For the first 30 days: every reply requires agent approval (Shadow Mode)
- After 30 days: replies above 0.78 confidence go autonomous; below that, queued for review
- Agent can always edit before sending

**Key capabilities:**
- Grounded in property-specific knowledge — does not make up availability or rates
- Shadow mode for safe rollout
- Confidence threshold control (you decide how autonomous it gets)
- Multi-language support (English, Hindi — more languages on roadmap)
- One-tap approve, edit, or reject

**Why it matters:**
> "Your Concierge currently works 9am–8pm and takes a day off. HAIP's AI Concierge replies in 90 seconds at 2am. The guest who messages at midnight gets an answer before they fall asleep — and before they book somewhere else."

**Shadow vs Autonomous Mode (important to explain correctly):**
- Shadow Mode = AI drafts every reply, agent approves before sending. Zero autonomous sends.
- Autonomous Mode = AI sends directly for high-confidence replies. Agent sees the log. Can override.
- We recommend 30 days of Shadow Mode minimum before any autonomy.

### Module 4: Leads360 — Unified Sales Pipeline

**What it does:**
Every enquiry from every channel becomes a Kanban card in a unified sales pipeline. One card per enquiry, one timeline per guest. The pipeline tracks the full funnel from first contact to booking.

**Pipeline stages:**
`NEW → RESPONDING → QUOTED → SITE_VISIT_SCHEDULED → SITE_VISIT_DONE → BOOKED → LOST / GHOSTED`

**Key capabilities:**
- Auto-creates inquiry card from WhatsApp, call, Instagram, or OTA
- Guest profile linked to every card (phone, email, booking history, preferences)
- AI-generated summary per inquiry (auto-updates when new messages or calls arrive)
- Manual override of AI summary with one-tap "Refresh Summary"
- Stage progression with reason logging (why was this inquiry lost?)
- Assignment to specific agents
- Check-in / check-out dates, party size, budget, occasion — all captured on the card
- Timeline view showing all messages, calls, and notes in one thread
- Source attribution (which channel originated this inquiry)

**Why it matters:**
> "Right now you have 40 active enquiries scattered across WhatsApp, Instagram, and someone's personal phone. You genuinely don't know which ones have been followed up. HAIP gives you one board. One number. Today you have 40 leads. 12 are hot. 6 have quotes out. 3 went quiet — here's how to re-engage them."

**What to show in demo:**
Show the Kanban board with columns. Click into an inquiry card to show guest profile, AI summary, and full conversation timeline in one view.

### Module 6: Automations

**What it does:**
Rule-based trigger/action automations that run in the background. When something happens (a new enquiry arrives, a call completes, a guest goes silent for 24 hours, a check-in is tomorrow), HAIP automatically takes action.

**7 Trigger types:**
1. `new_inquiry` — fires when any enquiry is created from any channel
2. `call_completed` — fires after every call is analysed and wrapped up
3. `no_response_2h` — fires when a guest message has had no agent reply for 2 hours
4. `no_response_24h` — fires when no reply for 24 hours
5. `inquiry_lost` — fires when an inquiry is moved to LOST stage
6. `stay_check_in_1d` — fires when a booked stay's check-in is tomorrow
7. `stay_check_out_1d` — fires when a booked stay's check-out is tomorrow

**5 Action types:**
1. `send_whatsapp_template` — send an approved WhatsApp template to the guest
2. `create_draft_reply` — create an AI-drafted reply queued for agent approval
3. `notify_team` — send a WhatsApp message to a specified team member
4. `move_to_followup` — automatically advance the inquiry stage from NEW → RESPONDING
5. `tag_guest` — add tags to the guest's subscriber profile

**Example automations that sell themselves:**
- "When a new enquiry arrives, automatically send our villa brochure template"
- "When a guest goes 24 hours without a reply, ping the sales manager on WhatsApp"
- "The day before check-in, automatically send our welcome and arrival info"
- "When a booking is lost, tag the guest as 'Lost-2026' for a re-engagement campaign later"

## 5. HOW HAIP WORKS

### The Guest Journey (end to end)

```
GUEST ACTION                     HAIP ACTION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Guest pings on WhatsApp      →   Message arrives in Unified Inbox
                                 → AI Concierge drafts reply
                                 → Agent approves with 1 tap
                                 → Inquiry card created in Leads360

Guest calls IVR              →   Call captured + consent recorded
                                 → Forwarded to agent's mobile
                                 → After call: transcribed by Sarvam
                                 → Claude generates wrap-up card
                                 → Disposition + next action suggested
                                 → Automation triggers if: callback needed

Guest goes quiet for 2h      →   Automation fires: notify manager
Guest goes quiet for 24h     →   Automation fires: send follow-up template

Inquiry reaches QUOTED stage  →   AI summary updates automatically
                                 → Agent can see full history in one view

Booking confirmed            →   Stay linked to guest GID (canonical ID)
                                 → Check-in automation scheduled
                                 → Pre-arrival WhatsApp sent D-1
                                 → Check-out automation scheduled
                                 → Post-stay re-engagement added to drip

Owner checks dashboard       →   Sees conversion rate, QA scores,
                                 channel mix, revenue attribution
```

### The Technology That Makes It Work (explain simply, not technically)

**"How does HAIP know what to reply?"**
> Every property uploads their room descriptions, rate cards, packages, house rules, F&B menu, and activity list. The AI is trained only on this. It does not make up rates or availability — it only replies based on what you've told it.

**"How do you get the call recordings?"**
> Your IVR provider (CallerDesk or Acefone) sends us a notification the moment a call ends. We download the recording from their servers, send it to an AI transcription service optimised for Indian accents, and have the wrap-up card ready in about 90 seconds.

**"How do you know who the guest is if they call from a new number?"**
> HAIP assigns every guest a unique identity (GID) and builds confidence from multiple signals — their phone number, WhatsApp account, booking ID, email, Instagram handle. If two signals match a known guest above an 85% confidence threshold, HAIP automatically recognises them as the same person. You can also manually merge two guest profiles.

**"What if a guest contacts us on WhatsApp and then calls?"**
> Both interactions appear on the same guest timeline automatically, linked by the guest identity system. The agent picking up the call can see the WhatsApp history before they say hello.

- What they are: Enterprise customer support platforms
- Why HAIP wins: Built for software companies, not hospitality. No IVR integration. No Indian accent transcription. No DPDP compliance. Way more expensive. Requires developers to configure.
- Objection: "Our head office already has Zendesk."
- Answer: "HAIP can sit beside Zendesk for your property-level communication — we handle the frontline guest touchpoints that Zendesk was never built for."

**WhatsApp CRM tools (Wati, Gallabox, Interakt)**

- Why HAIP wins: WhatsApp-only. No call intelligence. No Instagram. No IVR. No QA scoring. No sales pipeline. No hospitality-specific AI.

## 12. COMMON QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

### "How does HAIP get our call recordings?"

HAIP integrates directly with your IVR provider (CallerDesk or Acefone). When a call ends, your IVR provider sends a notification to HAIP with the call details. HAIP then downloads the recording from their servers, sends it for transcription, and generates the wrap-up card. Your agents don't do anything — it happens automatically within 90 seconds of the call ending.

---

### "Do we need to change our phone numbers?"

No. Your existing phone numbers stay exactly as they are. Your IVR provider continues to handle call routing. HAIP connects to your IVR account via API — it's invisible to your guests.

---

### "How does the WhatsApp integration work? We already have WhatsApp Business."

HAIP connects to WhatsApp via the official Meta Cloud API. This is different from the WhatsApp Business App on your phone. It requires a WhatsApp Business API account (also called a WABA). We walk you through the embedded signup process — it takes about 20 minutes and Meta approval typically takes 2–5 business days. Once connected, all your WhatsApp messages flow into HAIP's unified inbox. Your team no longer needs WhatsApp Web — they work from HAIP.

---

### "What happens to existing guest conversations on WhatsApp?"

Historical messages before the integration date will not be imported (Meta's API doesn't allow this). Going forward, all new messages appear in HAIP. Your team can still reference old conversations in WhatsApp Business App if needed.

---

### "Can we use our own WhatsApp number?"

Yes — HAIP uses your existing WhatsApp Business number, or we help you register a new one. The number stays yours. If you ever leave HAIP, you keep the number.

---

### "Is guest data safe? Where is it stored?"

All data is stored in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1). Call recordings are encrypted at rest using AWS KMS with per-tenant encryption keys. Guest data is isolated per property — your property's data is completely separate from any other HAIP client's data. HAIP is built to be DPDP Act compliant from day one.

---

### "What happens to our data if we cancel?"

You retain access to your data for 90 days after cancellation. During that period you can export guest profiles, conversation history, and call records. After 90 days, data is purged per our retention policy. You own your data.

---

### "Can different staff members have different levels of access?"

Yes. HAIP has role-based access control. Roles include: Owner (full access), Manager (team management + analytics), Agent (inbox + pipeline only), Compliance (audit log + DPDP tools). For multi-property accounts, you can scope each team member to specific properties — a FOH agent at Property A cannot see Property B's guest data.

---

### "Does the AI Concierge work in Hindi?"

The AI Concierge replies in English by default. Hindi support is on the roadmap for Q3 2026. The transcription system (Sarvam AI) already handles Hindi and English-Hindi code-switching in calls.

---

### "How long does it take to set up?"

For a single-property client with CallerDesk or Acefone: 14 days from contract to go-live. The critical path is WhatsApp Business API approval from Meta (2–5 business days). Everything else — IVR integration, user accounts, knowledge base — can be done in parallel.

---

### "What if we're not happy after 30 days?"

We don't have long-term lock-in. Month-to-month. If after 30 days the platform isn't delivering value, we'll work with you to identify why — and if we can't fix it, we'll cancel with no penalty. Our current clients are renewing, so we're confident in that offer.

---

### "Can HAIP work without WhatsApp?"

HAIP can work without the WhatsApp marketing module, but most of our value is delivered through WhatsApp (AI Concierge, unified inbox, campaigns). If your property doesn't use WhatsApp at all, HAIP's primary value would be call intelligence and the Leads360 pipeline. That's still valuable — but we'd recommend exploring whether adding WhatsApp makes sense for your guests.

---

### "Do you integrate with Booking.com or Airbnb directly?"

Booking.com and Airbnb messaging is synced via Hostaway (if you use Hostaway as your PMS). Direct Booking.com / Airbnb API adapters are on the H2 2026 roadmap.

---

### "We have a head office in Delhi. Can they see all properties?"

Yes. Users with Owner or Manager role can see all properties in the account. Property-scoped users only see their assigned properties. The analytics dashboard can filter by property or show portfolio-wide data.

## 13. GLOSSARY

**GID (HAIP Guest ID)**
The canonical identity for every guest in HAIP. A system-generated UUID. Every guest gets one GID regardless of how many channels they've contacted you through. Multiple signals (phone, WhatsApp, email, booking ID) are linked to a single GID.

**Signal**
Any piece of information that helps identify a guest: phone number, WhatsApp account, email address, Instagram handle, booking ID. Signals have confidence scores — phone: 90%, booking ID: 95%, email: 75%.

**Shadow Mode**
AI Concierge operational mode where all AI-drafted replies must be approved by a human agent before sending. Nothing reaches the guest automatically. Recommended for the first 30 days.

**Autonomous Mode**
AI Concierge mode where replies above a configured confidence threshold (default: 0.78) are sent directly to guests without agent review. Replies below the threshold are queued for agent review.

**Leads360**
HAIP's unified sales pipeline. One Kanban card per inquiry. Tracks every enquiry from first contact through to booking or loss.

**Inquiry**
A guest's interest in booking — could originate from WhatsApp, Instagram DM, phone call, OTA, web form, or walk-in. One inquiry = one Leads360 card. A guest can have multiple inquiries.

**Pipeline Stage**
Where an inquiry sits in the sales funnel: NEW → RESPONDING → QUOTED → SITE_VISIT_SCHEDULED → SITE_VISIT_DONE → BOOKED → LOST / GHOSTED.

**IVR (Interactive Voice Response)**
The phone system that routes incoming calls. HAIP integrates with CallerDesk (common for single-property villa owners) and Acefone (common for boutique hotels and multi-property groups).

**Wrap-up Card**
The AI-generated record of a phone call — includes summary, disposition suggestion, QA scores, and missed revenue flags. Created automatically within ~90 seconds of call end.

**Disposition**
HAIP's classification of how a call ended: Hot (ready to book), Quoted (rate given), Booked (confirmed), Lost (booking didn't happen), Callback (needs follow-up), No Answer, Voicemail.

**QA Score**
AI-generated quality assessment of a phone call. Scored on dimensions like: rate quoting accuracy, upsell attempts made, closing technique, tone and professionalism.

**Missed Revenue Flag**
An AI-generated alert when a call analysis detects that an upsell or revenue opportunity was not offered. Example: "Guest asked about private dining but agent did not offer the chef's table package."

**Automation Rule**
A HAIP rule that automatically fires an action when a trigger condition is met. Example: when a new inquiry arrives (trigger) → send the villa brochure WhatsApp template (action).

**WhatsApp Message Template**
A pre-approved WhatsApp message format registered with Meta. Required for any proactive (outbound) WhatsApp message sent to a guest. Templates are approved by Meta before use.

**WABA (WhatsApp Business Account)**
A business-grade WhatsApp account connected to the official Meta Cloud API. Required for HAIP's WhatsApp features. Different from the standard WhatsApp Business App.

**Campaign**
A bulk broadcast of a WhatsApp Message Template to a segmented list of subscribers. Sent in batches to comply with Meta rate limits.

**Subscriber**
A guest contact who has opted in to receive WhatsApp communications from the property.

**DPDP**
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Governs how businesses collect, store, and process personal data of Indian residents. HAIP is built to be compliant from day one.

**Tenant**
In HAIP's multi-tenant architecture, each property group is a tenant — a fully isolated environment with its own data, users, and settings. One client = one tenant.

**Celery**
HAIP's background task queue (technical term — for internal use). When we say "tasks run in the background" — this is the system that handles that. Relevant when clients ask why something (like a campaign) runs asynchronously.

**Sarvam AI**
HAIP's primary call transcription provider — an Indian AI company whose models are optimised for Indian English accents and English-Hindi code-switching. Why this matters: generic transcription tools (like Google or AWS) perform poorly on Indian accents. Sarvam is 30–40% more accurate on Indian hotel calls.
