# Privacy Notice (English) — Version 2 — Effective 1 June 2025

**HAIP Hospitality AI Intelligence Platform** ("we", "our", "HAIP") collects and processes your personal data as described below. This notice is provided under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

## Data we collect
- **Contact information:** phone number, name, email address
- **Communication records:** WhatsApp messages, call recordings, call transcripts
- **Stay and booking data:** check-in/check-out dates, property preferences, stay history
- **Interaction analytics:** AI-generated sentiment, intent, and quality scores

## Why we process your data
- To respond to your accommodation inquiries and booking requests
- To provide customer support via WhatsApp, phone, and other channels
- To improve service quality through AI-assisted call analysis
- To send marketing communications (only with your explicit consent)

## Your rights under the DPDP Act
You have the right to:
1. **Access** your personal data we hold
2. **Correct** inaccurate data
3. **Erase** your data (right to be forgotten) — requests processed within 30 days
4. **Withdraw consent** at any time by replying STOP to any WhatsApp message
5. **Nominate** a person to exercise rights on your behalf
6. **Grievance redressal** — contact our Grievance Officer (details below)

## Data retention
Call recordings: 12 months · Messages: 36 months · All other data: 24 months.
After this period, data is automatically deleted.

## Grievance Officer
For any privacy concern or data rights request, contact:
**Grievance Officer** · privacy@haip.in · Response within 30 days.
