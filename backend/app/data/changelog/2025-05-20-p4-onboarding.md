## What's new

**Onboarding now skippable** — Steps 4-9 (IVR, team invites, concierge mode, DPDP) can be set up later from Settings. Only brand + property + WhatsApp are required to get to dashboard.

**Command palette** — Press `Cmd+K` (or `Ctrl+K` on Windows) anywhere in the dashboard to search pages, guests, conversations, and trigger actions.

**Global status bar** — A slim bar at the top of the dashboard shows WhatsApp / IVR / AI health. Turns red and alerts when something breaks.

**In-app changelog** — You're reading it! Click the 🎁 icon in the top bar to see what's new.

**Help drawer** — Click the `?` icon to open video walkthroughs and a direct WhatsApp support link.

**IVR providers ranked** — Onboarding step 3 now shows Acefone first (most popular for Indian hospitality), with a "Not sure?" link to chat with our team.
