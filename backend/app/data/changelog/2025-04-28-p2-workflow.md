## What's new

**Scheduled send** — Click the ↓ chevron next to Send to schedule a message for later. Preset options (1h, tomorrow 9am, Monday) or pick a custom time.

**Bulk actions** — Select multiple conversations with checkboxes (hover to reveal). Bulk resolve or mark as read.

**Agent presence** — Green dot + avatar stack in the left panel shows who's online now. Heartbeat every 30s.

**@mention teammates** — Type `@name` in the composer to mention a teammate. They'll see the note in their queue.

**Transcript bookmarks** — Star any transcript turn to bookmark it. Bookmarks panel in call detail for quick review.

**Per-turn coaching notes** — Click the comment icon on any transcript turn to add a private coaching note.

**Saved views (Inbox + Inquiries)** — Save filter combos as named views. Switch with one click.

**Round-robin assignment** — New inquiries are auto-assigned to the next available team member in rotation.
